---
title: ONE YEAR AFTER MY FIRST STEAM RELEASE: WAS IT WORTH IT?
date: August 1st, 2026
author: Murilo M. Grosso
---

It’s been exactly one year since I released my first commercial game on Steam, <span class="colored-text">[The Warrior](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2411250/The_Warrior/)</span>. Was it worth it? Let’s check the numbers.

I won’t share the exact data, but I’ll use publicly available information that roughly reflects the actual numbers.

According to [Video Game Insights](https://app.sensortower.com/vgi/), the game has sold over 11,000 copies and generated an estimated $18.1k in net revenue. Of course, that’s before accounting for publisher cuts and taxes (which deserve a post of their own).

<figure>
    <img src="imgs/thewarrior_videogameinsights.jpg" alt="Video Game Insights data">
    <figcaption class="muted-text">Video Game Insights estimates for The Warrior</figcaption>
</figure>

The game has also received 315 Steam reviews, 97% of which are positive.

Honestly, for a small hobby project made by an unknown studio, this is far more than I ever expected. It's a very significant achievement for a solo developer's first game, and enough to fund the development of my next project!

As encouraging as those numbers are, my biggest gain was undoubtedly the experience itself.

I learned how to manage a game after launch, how to use reviews to update and improve it, what players like and don’t like, the importance of discounts and festivals, and how Steam and the industry in general work.

During this period, the game was also played by amazing streamers around the globe ([I watched every video and livestream I could find](https://youtube.com/playlist?list=PLzAMMdxbxDKDO6SLeA7OuIkY8AuR0TUQT&si=X_QkPvhM99dEMqQ0)) and was even selected as a finalist at [Gamescom](https://latam.gamescom.global/)!

    <img src="imgs/gamescom_20260528.jpg" alt="Daniel and Murilo at Gamescom">

Living as an indie developer feels much more achievable than I ever anticipated, and I'm now confident that, with enough dedication and the experience I've gained, I can turn this hobby into a full-time career!

As always, a huge thank you to everyone who played and supported the game over the past year!
