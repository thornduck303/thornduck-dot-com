WHY SHOULD YOU START A BLOG?

July 29th, 2026

For years, I've been trying to start a devlog series on my YouTube channel.

While I did manage to post one video a while ago, creating a video I'm truly happy with takes a lot of time and effort.

Don't get me wrong, I enjoy the process. But as a hobbyist game developer, my free time is limited, and I'd rather spend it making games than editing videos.

That said, I still really enjoy sharing my thoughts and development process.

Inspired by some friends of mine, I think I've found a solution: starting a blog!

Why a blog? Well, simply put, it's much easier to write a blog post than to produce a video (you still have to write a script either way, but I can skip the recording and editing part). That's it.

So, what am I going to post here?

Anything related to my game development journey, of course!

I want to keep each post short and focused. If I ever find myself dragging things out too much in a single post, I'll just split it into two.

I also don't want to stick to a fixed schedule. I'll write whenever I have something worth sharing and feel like sitting down to write.

So... that's it. Let's see how it goes!

In just a couple of days, it'll be exactly a year since my first Steam release, The Warrior, so I plan to write about that next.

Thanks for stopping by!

- Murilo M. Grosso
